## Why is an "implant only" price so different from a complete tooth?

An implant is the titanium screw that sits in the bone. On its own it doesn't replace a tooth: you also need an abutment and a crown on top. Many clinics advertise the implant-only price. Always ask for the price of the **complete tooth**, which at our clinic is ₹35,000 with a ceramic crown.

## Why basal implants are priced per implant

Basal implants are longer implants that anchor in the dense cortical bone of the jaw. They are used when there isn't enough bone for a conventional implant, and they can carry fixed teeth within 72 hours. Because they replace the need for bone grafting (which can add ₹20,000 to ₹60,000 per site elsewhere), the total cost for someone with bone loss is often lower even though the per-implant price is higher.

## What makes implant prices vary between clinics

1. **Implant brand.** Korean and Indian implants cost less than premium European brands. Ask which brand you are getting.
2. **Crown material.** Metal, ceramic and zirconia crowns differ in price and looks.
3. **Bone grafting.** If a clinic needs to graft before placing implants, the cost and time rise sharply.
4. **What is included.** Scans, temporary teeth and follow-ups are sometimes charged separately.
5. **Experience.** An experienced implantologist plans more precisely, which lowers the risk of failure and repeat costs.

## Questions to ask before you choose a clinic

- Is the quote for the implant only, or the complete tooth?
- Which implant brand will be used?
- Is a CBCT scan included?
- Will I need a bone graft? If so, what does it cost?
- What warranty do you give, and what does it cover?
